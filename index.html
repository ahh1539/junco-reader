Junco Reader

Read any PDF out loud in your browser. Free, private, on-device Kokoro text-to-speech. No account required. Your documents stay on your device.

How it works

  1. Drop a PDF, TXT, or Markdown file, or paste text.
  2. Download the on-device Kokoro voice model once (optional cache).
  3. Press play — narration runs entirely in your browser.

Privacy

  • No accounts or uploads of your documents.
  • Parsing and speech synthesis happen locally.
  • The only optional network fetch after page load (besides fonts) is the voice model when you choose to download it.
