Junco Reader vs Offline-TTS

Both are free, installable, on-device Kokoro readers with no account required. Here's where they differ in practice.

  Junco Reader offline-tts.com
Cost Free, no ads Free, no ads
Voice before any download Yes -- your browser's built-in voice plays instantly No -- the ~300 MB Kokoro model must download first
Neural voice engine Kokoro-82M (on-device) Kokoro (on-device)
File formats PDF, TXT, Markdown, pasted text Pasted or typed text (no file upload, as far as we can tell)
Documents leave your device Never Never
Installable (PWA) Yes Yes
Companion app Junco for iPhone -- newsletters as a daily podcast None

Where offline-tts.com is ahead

Not much separates the two once the model's downloaded -- offline-tts.com was also early to make itself installable as a home-screen app, which is worth crediting. If all you need is to paste a paragraph and hear it back offline afterward, it does that cleanly.

Where Junco Reader is ahead

You can drop in a PDF or Markdown file, not just paste text -- and you're never forced to wait on the 300 MB download to hear something: switch to Junco Reader's Instant tier for your browser's built-in voice with zero download, no gate to clear first. Junco Reader is also built by the team behind Junco, an iPhone app that turns your email newsletters into a short daily podcast using the same family of on-device voices.

Try it yourself -- drop in a PDF or paste an article, and switch to the Instant tier if you don't want to wait on a download.
